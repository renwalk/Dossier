CURRICULUMVITAE

Overview

As a visual communication designer, researcher, and educator, I explore how design can deepen our understanding of the world through multisensory engagement, data-driven methods, and inclusive practices. My work bridges studio practice and scholarship, with ongoing research in information design, typography, and systems thinking across disciplines.
